# Channel Study #2 — Beyond Rep Theory (measured 2026-11-24, yt-dlp catalog pulls)

All numbers measured from live catalogs this date. Raw JSON was in /tmp/niche (ephemeral).

## Yellow Dude — 1.71M subs, 144 videos, 106.7M views, median 303k/video, top-3 = 14% of views
The real giant of the animated format — consistent hits, not one-hit. Top: "What actually makes muscles grow" 5.5M (6:44); "Why your tendons never heal" 5.1M (5:30); "Why you're not losing fat in the areas you want" 4.4M; "Get 6-pack abs faster with this simple morning routine" 3.7M; "Quit gym, do calisthenics" 3.4M; "The laziest way to build muscle" 3.1M; "I did dead hang for 30 days… here's what happened" 2.6M. Hits run 5–7 min. Only flops = sponsor product videos (3.7k–7.3k).

## Gravity Transformation — 5.9M subs, 587 videos, 924.8M views, median 456k/video, top-3 = 8%
Consistency king. Industrial formats: numbered listicles ("20 Foods That Have Almost 0 Calories" 21M; "10 HIIT Exercises to Lose Belly Fat FASTER" 29M), "What Happens After 30 Days of Cold Showers" 17M, fat-loss everything. 12–17 min videos. Recent videos still 234k–592k.

## Body Hub — 1.52M subs, 1,341 videos, 160M views, median 11k (decaying)
Classics: "What will happen if you plank every day for 1 minute" 20M (3:40); "5 Foods To Eat To Quickly Gain Weight and Muscle" 8.5M; "5 ways to gain healthy weight for skinny people" 3.5M; "Do 50 Push Ups Every Morning and See What Happens To Your Body" 3.1M; "If you're skinny, this is how to quickly gain muscle" 2.8M. Recent videos collapsed to 4.8k–33k — including "Unfortunately, Training Tendons Gives You Insane Strength" at 7.1k (tendon-strength flop on a big channel, Nov 2026).

## Greythenics — 14.6k subs, 22 videos, median 4.3k, top-3 = 86%
Direct animated clone. Breakouts: "Skinny fat? Here's what you actually need to do" 338k (3:18); "Train like this if you want a demon back" 284k. Recent videos 1.1k–1.7k — clone fading fast without a differentiated system.

## Calisthenics with Lavender — 86k subs, 57 videos, median 10k, top-3 = 60%
Women's bend of the same format. Breakouts are fat-loss/routine intent: belly fat 898k, bedtime routine 847k, "6 Science-Backed Ways Women Should Train Calisthenics" 484k.

## Signals
- Tendon-STRENGTH is cooling: Rep Theory momentum 0.18x (OutlierKit), Body Hub tendon video flopped. Tendon PAIN/HEALING is hot (Yellow Dude #2 all-time, 5.1M).
- "Skinny fat" identity = proven breakout, untouched by Rep Theory and by us.
- Skinny GAINING (weight gain for skinny people) = multi-million proven demand, ignored by Rep Theory (strength-only).
- 30-day self-experiment format proven at every scale (2.6M / 4.3M / 17M).
- Short "what happens if you do X daily" videos (3–4 min) hold the highest ceiling in the study (20M).
- Fat loss is the biggest raw demand pool in the niche; our strength-only positioning ignores it.
